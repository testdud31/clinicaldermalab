1. Executive Clinical Summary & Overview

Vitamin C (L-ascorbic acid) is universally acknowledged in medical dermatology as the single most critical topical antioxidant for preserving epidermal integrity. However, formulating a stable, bioavailable, and deeply penetrating vitamin C solution remains one of skincare chemistry's greatest hurdles. Obagi Medical Professional-C Serum 20% represents the clinical gold standard in high-concentration antioxidant delivery.

Tailored specifically for normal to oily skin profiles, this prescription-grade over-the-counter formulation pairs 20% L-ascorbic acid with a stabilizing hydro-glycolic vehicle and sodium hyaluronate. Unlike water-heavy cosmetic serums that rapidly oxidize into ineffective dehydroascorbic acid, Obagi's micro-penetration technology ensures that active vitamin C passes through the hydrophobic stratum corneum directly into the viable epidermis and upper dermis.

In comprehensive laboratory testing, daily morning application of Obagi Professional-C 20% demonstrated profound photoprotection against reactive oxygen species (ROS) induced by ultraviolet radiation (UVA/UVB), high-energy visible (HEV) blue light, and atmospheric ozone pollution.

6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What skin types benefit most from the 20% concentration? ▼
Obagi Professional-C 20% is specifically engineered for normal to oily skin and experienced vitamin C users seeking maximum hyperpigmentation reduction and collagen stimulation. Individuals with hyper-reactive or dry skin may prefer starting with Obagi 10% or 15% before progressing to the 20% formulation.
What color should the serum be when fresh? ▼
Fresh Obagi Professional-C Serum 20% ranges from transparent clear to pale straw-yellow. Over 3 to 6 months of daily oxygen exposure, it will gradually shift to a deeper amber. If the serum turns dark mahogany or cloudy brown, the L-ascorbic acid has oxidized and should be replaced.
How many drops should I apply and when? ▼
Apply 5 to 7 drops once daily in the morning immediately after cleansing. Gently press across the face, neck, and chest using your fingertips. Always follow with a broad-spectrum mineral sunscreen like EltaMD UV Clear SPF 46.
Can I use Obagi Professional-C with retinol? ▼
Yes, but not at the exact same moment. Apply Obagi Professional-C Serum in the morning for environmental antioxidant defense, and use your prescribed retinol or retinoid in the evening for nocturnal cellular turnover.
