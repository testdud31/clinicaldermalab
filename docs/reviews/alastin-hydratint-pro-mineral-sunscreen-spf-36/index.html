Biochemical Formulation & Photoprotection Audit

Our dermatology board audited ALASTIN HydraTint Pro across four clinical pillars: physical UV attenuation, visible light defense, antioxidant stability, and barrier compatibility:

1. Dual Physical Filters (Titanium Dioxide 8.9% + Zinc Oxide 3.4%)

Titanium Dioxide delivers high-efficiency UVB and short-wave UVA2 protection, while Zinc Oxide extends attenuation into long-wave UVA1—the primary driver of dermal elastosis and photoaging—with zero chemical-filter eye stinging.

2. Iron Oxides (HEV Blue Light Shield)

Clinical photobiology confirms that High-Energy Visible (HEV) blue light worsens melasma and post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation. The mineral iron oxides in HydraTint Pro provide a physical visible-light barrier while neutralizing redness.

3. Ectoin (Extremolyte Osmoprotectant)

Ectoin binds a protective hydration shell around epidermal cell membranes, shielding keratinocytes from oxidative stress caused by urban particulate pollution (PM2.5), infrared heat, and UV exposure.

4. Squalane & Fragrance-Free 2-in-1 Emulsion

Biomimetic squalane and glycerin deliver weightless, non-comedogenic hydration so normal-to-combination skin can skip a separate morning cream. Zero synthetic fragrance or essential oils ensures compatibility with rosacea and post-laser skin.

Dermatologist Morning Application Protocol

  1. Step 1 — Prep & Brighten: After gentle cleansing, apply your morning antioxidant or brightening step (such as JIYU Korean Snail Mucin Toner Pads) and allow 60 seconds to absorb.
  2. Step 2 — Optional Barrier Layer: Normal-to-oily skin can wear HydraTint Pro as a standalone 2-in-1 moisturizer + SPF. Dry or retinoid-sensitized skin should layer La Roche-Posay Toleriane Double Repair underneath.
  3. Step 3 — Full 2-Finger Dose: Apply 2–3 pumps across the face and 1 pump on the neck 15 minutes before sun exposure. Blend evenly; the sheer tint sets in 45 seconds.
  4. Step 4 — Reapplication: Reapply every 2 hours during direct sun exposure or after 40 minutes of swimming or heavy sweating.

Frequently Asked Dermatologist Questions

Is ALASTIN HydraTint Pro Mineral SPF 36 good for melasma and hyperpigmentation?

Yes. Combining physical mineral UV filters (Titanium Dioxide and Zinc Oxide) with tinted Iron Oxides shields against both UV radiation and High-Energy Visible (HEV) blue light—a primary trigger for melasma and dark spots.

Does ALASTIN HydraTint Pro leave a white cast or feel greasy?

No. The universal iron-oxide tint neutralizes mineral chalkiness and dries down to a natural, luminous satin-glow finish without heavy grease.

How does it compare to Colorescience Face Shield Flex SPF 50?

ALASTIN HydraTint Pro offers a larger 3.2 oz bottle, sheerer universal glow tint, and 2-in-1 moisturizing comfort with Ectoin, whereas Colorescience Face Shield Flex SPF 50 provides higher SPF 50 protection, 12% Zinc Oxide, and buildable medium coverage across multiple shade tiers.

Is ALASTIN HydraTint Pro safe for sensitive, acne-prone, or post-procedure skin?

Yes. It is 100% mineral, fragrance-free, paraben-free, and non-comedogenic, making it widely recommended after microneedling, chemical peels, and laser resurfacing.
