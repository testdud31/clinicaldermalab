Biochemical Formulation & Active Ingredient Audit

1. Snail Secretion Filtrate (Mucin)

Rich in glycosaminoglycans and allantoin, snail mucin binds epidermal water, accelerates micro-barrier recovery, and visibly plumps dehydration wrinkles.

2. Niacinamide (Vitamin B3)

Inhibits melanosome transfer to gradually fade post-inflammatory dark spots while stimulating ceramide synthesis to strengthen the lipid barrier.

3. Multi-Peptide Complex & Centella Asiatica

Biomimetic peptides support dermal firmness while Centella triterpenoids suppress subclinical inflammation, making daily twice-a-day use comfortable even for reactive skin.
