Executive Summary: Biochemical Enzymatic Resurfacing

Stratum corneum turnover slows naturally by up to 50% between youth and mature adulthood. When dead, keratinized corneocytes fail to detach from the skin surface, they create an irregular micro-relief that scatters incident light—causing the skin to appear dull, sallow, and coarse. Furthermore, compacted cellular debris clogs follicular funnels, accentuating enlarged pores and causing liquid makeup to settle unevenly.

While traditional high-strength chemical peels (such as 10–20% Glycolic Acid) can dislodge this layer, their aggressive acidity (pH < 3.2) frequently causes erythema, stinging, and micro-inflammation in sensitive or barrier-impaired patients.

ELEMIS Dynamic Resurfacing Facial Pads (ASIN B0D3JK3D39) sidestep acid trauma by employing Patented Tri-Enzyme Technology. Instead of dissolving the stratum corneum indiscriminately, biological enzymes cleave peptide bonds selectively in dead protein structures:

  • Phase 1: Enzymatic Desmosome Cleavage: A synergistic triad of Papain, Subtilisin, and Protease breaks down the corneodesmosomal anchors that bind dead surface cells together.
  • Phase 2: Gentle Alpha-Hydroxy Acid Polishing: Mild Lactic Acid gently promotes cellular turnover while operating as a natural humectant to preserve the Natural Moisturizing Factor (NMF).
  • Phase 3: Microbiome Rebalancing: A Probiotic Ferment Complex nourishes beneficial cutaneous microflora, preventing post-exfoliation barrier compromise.
  • Phase 4: Physical Lifting: The woven embossed waffle texture of the pad sweeps away lifted debris mechanically without micro-fissuring the stratum corneum.

Biochemical Formulation Analysis: The Tri-Enzyme Mechanism

Our analytical breakdown reveals why ELEMIS's Tri-Enzyme system provides clinical exfoliation without acid-induced barrier damage:

1. Papain (Proteolytic Papaya Extract)

Derived from the green fruit of Carica papaya, Papain is an endopeptidase enzyme that hydrolyzes internal peptide bonds in keratin proteins. It targets dead, denatured corneocyte proteins on the epidermal surface, digesting compacted keratin without altering living epidermal layers.

2. Subtilisin & Protease Biocompatible Enzymes

Subtilisin is a fermentation-derived serine endopeptidase that acts synergistically with Protease to mimic the skin’s natural peeling enzymes (Kallikrein-5 and Kallikrein-7). As we age, natural desquamation enzymes decline; this biological complex restores youthful enzymatic desquamation rates without burning or peeling.

3. Lactic Acid (AHA) Multi-Tasking Keratolytic

Lactic acid boasts a larger molecular weight than glycolic acid, meaning it penetrates more uniformly across the stratum corneum with zero hot-spot stinging. Beyond exfoliating, Lactic Acid converts into lactate, a vital component of the skin's Natural Moisturizing Factor (NMF) that hydrates newly revealed cells.

4. Probiotic Ferment Complex (Lactobacillus Lysate)

Post-exfoliation skin is uniquely susceptible to environmental disruption. The addition of bio-fermented Lactobacillus provides postbiotics and peptidoglycans that calm inflammatory pathways and strengthen the stratum corneum barrier against dehydration.

Frequently Asked Questions: Tri-Enzyme Resurfacing Q&A

Can you use ELEMIS Dynamic Resurfacing Facial Pads daily on sensitive skin?

Yes. While conventional high-percentage glycolic acid pads can cause erythema or chemical burns on sensitive skin, ELEMIS utilizes biologically targeted Tri-Enzyme technology (Papain, Subtilisin, Protease) paired with mild Lactic Acid. Sensitive skin types should begin with once daily application in the evening, graduating to morning and night as tolerance allows.

How does the patented Tri-Enzyme technology differ from standard AHA/BHA chemical peels?

Chemical acid peels depend on low pH levels (pH 3.0–3.5) to indiscriminately burn intercellular bonds, which frequently compromises healthy lipid barriers. In contrast, ELEMIS's patented Tri-Enzymes specifically target and break peptide bonds in un-desquamated dead keratin cells at skin-friendly pH (4.5–5.5), leaving viable living epidermal cells and intercellular ceramides completely untouched.

Which side of the pad should be applied to the skin first?

Use the textured, embossed waffle side first in gentle circular sweeping motions across the forehead, nose, cheeks, and chin to physically lift loosened cellular debris. Then flip the pad to the smooth side to glide the remaining Tri-Enzyme essence and probiotic ferment evenly over the face and neck.

Can I use these resurfacing pads in the same routine as Retinol or Tretinoin?

We recommend skin cycling. Use the ELEMIS Dynamic Resurfacing Pads in the morning or on alternate non-retinoid evenings. Combining potent keratolytic enzymes and topical prescription retinoids in the exact same application can accelerate epidermal turnover too quickly, leading to temporary peeling.
