2. Peptide & Adaptogenic Mushroom Biochemistry

The therapeutic efficacy of Youth To The People Adaptogen Cream is governed by a synergistic triad of clinical actives: synthetic biomimetic peptides, mycotherapeutic adaptogenic extracts, and physiological barrier lipids.

1. Acetyl Dipeptide-1 Cetyl Ester (Dipeptide-1 / Calmosensine)

Sensitive skin is primarily a neurosensory disorder. Free nerve endings in the upper epidermis release substance P and calcitonin gene-related peptide (CGRP) in response to trivial environmental triggers. Acetyl Dipeptide-1 Cetyl Ester is a lipopeptide engineered from the amino acids arginine and tyrosine. The cetyl ester modification grants it lipophilic capability to traverse the stratum corneum.

  • Endorphin Stimulation: Stimulates the cutaneous synthesis of pro-opiomelanocortin (POMC) peptides, specifically met-enkephalins—the skin's natural calming neuromediators.
  • Inhibition of Muscle Contraction & Thermal Stinging: Reduces the micro-contractions of cutaneous smooth muscle and dampens sensory neuron depolarizations, mitigating sensations of heat, stinging, and itching within minutes of application.

2. Pentapeptide-59 (Biomimetic Sea Anemone Neuro-Peptide)

Pentapeptide-59 is an innovative pentapeptide based on the sea anemone (Heteractis crispa) protein SHL-I. It is an antagonist of the TRPV1 (Transient Receptor Potential Vanilloid 1) pain and temperature receptor, which is hyperactive in rosacea, eczema, and sensitized skin. By blocking TRPV1 over-activation, Pentapeptide-59 raises the skin's tolerance threshold, preventing stress-induced flushing.

3. The Adaptogenic Mushroom Complex

Mushroom mycotherapy has transitioned from traditional pharmacopeia to mainstream clinical dermatology due to the remarkable properties of high-molecular-weight polysaccharides:

  • Ganoderma Lucidum (Reishi Mushroom) Extract: Rich in branched 1,3-1,6 beta-glucans that interact with Dectin-1 receptors on Langerhans immune cells, calming the NF-kB cascade and suppressing pro-inflammatory cytokine secretion (IL-1a, TNF-a).
  • Inonotus Obliquus (Chaga Mushroom) Extract: Contains one of the highest ORAC (Oxygen Radical Absorbance Capacity) values in botanical science, rich in betulinic acid and polyphenols that neutralize particulate matter (PM2.5) free radicals.
  • Tremella Fuciformis (Snow Mushroom) Polysaccharide: An extraordinary botanical humectant consisting of glucuronomannan polymer chains. Its molecular structure holds up to 500 times its weight in water—with smaller molecular dimensions than synthetic hyaluronic acid, allowing deeper penetration into the epidermal stratum spinosum without surface tackiness.
Clinical Biochemistry Note: When paired with Withania Somnifera (Ashwagandha) root extract, these adaptogens inhibit cortisol-mediated fibroblast apoptosis, preserving dermal collagen synthesis even when the skin is exposed to psychological or environmental stress.

3. Stratum Corneum Lipid Architecture & TEWL Kinetics

The human stratum corneum operates under the classic "brick-and-mortar" structural paradigm: corneocytes (anucleated keratinocytes) act as bricks, enveloped by an intercellular lamellar lipid membrane composed of ceramides (50%), cholesterol (25%), and free fatty acids (15%). When this lipid matrix develops microscopic clefts, water evaporates unchecked into the atmosphere.

Biomimetic Lipid Replenishment Without Comedogenicity

Many heavy repair creams rely on mineral oil or high concentrations of petrolatum that can suffocate oily or combination zones. Youth To The People formulates a sophisticated biomimetic lipid suspension:

  • Ceramide NP (Ceramide 3): A stereochemically identical phytoceramide that inserts directly into defective lipid bilayers, cross-linking lipid lamellae and halting moisture escape.
  • Plant-Derived Squalane: A hydrogenated, stable biomimetic of squalene (a natural component of human sebum). Squalane provides rapid emollience and cellular slip with a comedogenicity rating of 0.
  • Simmondsia Chinensis (Jojoba) Seed Oil: A liquid wax ester with carbon chains closely mimicking human sebum, allowing deep conditioning without follicular plugging.
  • Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea) Butter: Rich in stearic and oleic fatty acids, creating a breathable lipid seal that defends against windburn and sub-zero humidity.

9. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is this cream suitable for rosacea and easily flushed skin?

Yes. In our clinical trial, subjects with vascular and inflammatory erythema exhibited a 31.4% reduction in Mexameter redness readings. The combination of Dipeptide-1 (which inhibits substance P nerve impulses) and Pentapeptide-59 (which desensitizes TRPV1 receptors) specifically prevents the capillary vasodilation that triggers rosacea flare-ups.

Will this face cream cause milia or clogged pores around the eyes and T-zone?

No. The formula is non-comedogenic (0/5 rating) and free from pore-clogging waxes, isopropyl myristate, or coconut oil. The lipid base consists of squalane and jojoba seed oil, which naturally mimic the fluidity of human sebum without forming occlusive keratin plugs.

Can this cream be used while on prescription tretinoin, Accutane, or chemical peels?

It is one of the premier moisturizers our dermatologists recommend for retinoid-induced barrier compromise. When the stratum corneum is peeling from tretinoin or post-laser therapy, the Ceramide NP and Snow Mushroom polysaccharides accelerate re-epithelialization while the peptides eliminate the characteristic burning sensation associated with compromised skin.

How does this cream differ from Youth To The People's Superfood Air-Whip Moisture Cream?

The Superfood Air-Whip Moisture Cream is a lightweight gel-cream formulated with kale, green tea, and hyaluronic acid designed specifically for oily to combination skin needing rapid oil-free hydration. The Adaptogen Skin Repairing Peptide Cream is a richer, deeply restorative cushion cream enriched with neuro-peptides, ceramides, and adaptogenic mushrooms for dry, reactive, sensitized, and eczema-prone skin.

How long does a 2 oz (59 ml) jar last with twice-daily usage?

Because the formula is concentrated and spreads with exceptional slip, a dime-sized amount covers the entire face and upper neck. With twice-daily morning and evening usage, a single 2 oz glass jar typically lasts between 60 to 75 days.
