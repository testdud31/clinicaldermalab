The 3-in-1 Physical Transformation: Phase Science

What sets the ELEMIS Pro-Collagen Cleansing Balm apart from standard liquid oil cleansers and solid petroleum waxes is its precisely engineered melting profile:

1

Phase 1: Rich Solid Balm (Room Temperature / In Jar)

Formulated with structured vegetable waxes (Mimosa and Rose) and hydrogenated triglycerides. It maintains structural stability at room temperature (preventing messy leakage during travel), but exhibits a low melting threshold calibrated precisely to body temperature (34°C – 37°C).

2

Phase 2: Ultra-Fluid Dissolution Oil (Friction & Warmth on Dry Skin)

Massaged over dry skin, the shear stress and cutaneous warmth liquefy the wax lattice into a rich lipophilic oil. Non-polar oils (Sweet Almond, Coconut, Starflower) wrap around waterproof silicones, sunscreen filters, and oxidized sebum, solubilizing debris without skin traction.

3

Phase 3: Hydrophilic Emulsifying Milk (Contact with Warm Water)

Incorporating non-ionic solubilizers (PEG-6 Caprylic/Capric Glycerides and PEG-60 Almond Glycerides), the formula undergoes instantaneous inversion upon contact with warm water. The lipid droplets micro-emulsify into a milky rinse that lifts away cleanly, preventing pore occlusion.

Biochemical Formulation Analysis: Marine & Botanical Actives

Behind the sensory spa experience lies a clinically potent cocktail of bioactives targeted at mature, barrier-compromised, and dry skin:

1. Padina Pavonica (Marine Brown Mediterranean Algae)

The hero marine active of ELEMIS's Pro-Collagen franchise. Extracted through sustainable harvesting, this Mediterranean fan-shaped brown macroalgae is rich in polysaccharides and calcium-binding glycosaminoglycans (GAGs). In vitro dermatological studies show Padina Pavonica promotes collagen synthesis and preserves hyaluronic acid in the extracellular matrix, delivering a visible plumping effect post-cleanse.

2. Starflower (Borage) Seed Oil: Rich in Gamma-Linolenic Acid (GLA)

Borage (*Borago officinalis*) seed oil contains the highest known natural concentration of Gamma-Linolenic Acid (GLA, 20–24%), an omega-6 polyunsaturated fatty acid crucial for maintaining the skin's lipid barrier. GLA directly downregulates cyclooxygenase (COX-2) pro-inflammatory cascades, making this cleansing balm remarkably soothing for reactive or rosacea-prone skin.

3. Elderberry Fruit Seed Oil (*Sambucus nigra*)

Extracted from English elderberries, this cold-pressed oil provides a rich ratio of Linoleic and Alpha-Linolenic acids (essential Omega-3 and Omega-6). It is fortified with anthocyanins and quercetin, providing cutaneous antioxidant defense against residual environmental particulate matter during the evening cleanse.

4. Optimega™ Botanical Lipid Complex

A proprietary bio-identical lipid matrix engineered from Wheatgerm and Oat lipids. Optimega™ mimics the natural lipid profile of the stratum corneum, compensating for the natural sebum loss that occurs during cleansing and ensuring skin feels supple, never taut.

5. Rose and Mimosa Floral Waxes

Unlike drying synthetic waxes, rose and mimosa waxes provide emollient slip that minimizes mechanical drag across sensitive facial capillaries while lending a delicate, natural calming aroma that turns daily hygiene into a stress-reducing ritual.

Frequently Asked Questions: Dermatologist Q&A

How do I properly use the included cleansing cloth?

After massaging the balm into an oil, run the dual-sided cloth under warm water and wring out excess. Hold the warm cloth over your face for 5 seconds to allow steam to loosen pores, then gently wipe away the emulsified oil. Rinse the cloth thoroughly and hang to dry.

Will this clog pores or cause cystic acne breakouts?

No. The lipid phase is formulated with non-comedogenic and low-comedogenic plant triglycerides that emulsify completely with water. However, if you are prone to congestion or cystic acne, always follow with a water-soluble foaming cleanser as part of a classic double cleanse.

Can I use ELEMIS Cleansing Balm to remove waterproof sunscreen?

Yes, this is its standout clinical strength. Mineral sunscreens containing high concentrations of Zinc Oxide and Titanium Dioxide are lipophilic and resist water. The oils in ELEMIS bind to these physical filters immediately, removing them in one effortless sweep.

What is the difference between the Original and Naked versions?

The Original formula features the signature calming aroma of lavender, eucalyptus, and chamomile essential oils. The "Naked" version contains the exact same Padina Pavonica and lipid foundation, but is 100% fragrance-free for individuals with fragrance contact allergies.
