Key Clinical Takeaways (Medical Policy)

Clinical Independence & Evidence-Based Peer-Review Protocols

  • •
    Zero Commercial Brand Sponsorships: Our laboratory purchases all cosmetic formulations at standard retail price. No skincare conglomerate provides gifted samples, free testing batches, or editorial pre-approval.
  • •
    Physician Sign-Off Requirement: No review or diagnostic condition monograph is published without primary author credential verification and final signature from our Chief Medical Officer.
  • •
    Continuous Biannual Audits: Product formulations are audited every 6 months to account for unannounced manufacturer reformulations, preservation changes, and active ingredient dilutions.

Rigorous Editorial Quality

Our 4-Stage Medical Peer Review Protocol

To eliminate confirmation bias and guarantee clinical accuracy, every product report navigates a sequential 4-stage pipeline before publication.

1

In-Vitro Screening

Formulation decomposition, active ingredient concentration assay via HPLC, and initial preservative system stability test.

2

Biometric Testing

Volunteer cohort testing evaluating TEWL reduction with Tewameter hex-probes, Corneometer hydration, and follicular biopsies.

3

Meta-Analysis

Statistical cross-correlation with longitudinal real-world patient data (tolerance rates, irritation flags, and adverse event logs).

4

Physician Sign-Off

Final editorial audit by Dr. Aris Thorne, MD, FAAD, ensuring statements align with current AAD dermatological treatment guidelines.
