Biochemical Formulation Analysis

Our analytical breakdown reveals the unique synergy between marine biotechnology and photoprotective chemistry:

1. Padina Pavonica (Mediterranean Brown Fan Macroalgae)

Harvested along the pristine Mediterranean coastline, Padina Pavonica's unique calcified fan thallus is rich in sulfated polysaccharides (fucans) and bioavailable minerals. In cell culture studies, these marine polysaccharides signal dermal fibroblasts to ramp up the endogenous synthesis of Glycosaminoglycans (GAGs) like hyaluronic acid. This expands the dermal interstitial matrix, resulting in visibly plumper, firmer skin and noticeable softening of expression lines.

2. Chlorella Vulgaris (Green Microalgae Matrix Protector)

Chlorella is a single-celled microalgae containing an extensive spectrum of essential amino acids, peptides, and chlorophyll. When exposed to daytime UV radiation, skin produces matrix metalloproteinases (MMPs) that sever collagen and elastin bonds. Chlorella peptides directly inhibit MMP-1 (collagenase) and elastase activity, preventing solar dermal collapse and loss of skin elasticity.

3. Ginkgo Biloba & Porphyridium Cruentum Red Algae

High-potency flavonoid antioxidants from Ginkgo Biloba protect against environmental urban ozone and blue-light ROS (Reactive Oxygen Species). Paired with red microalgae (*Porphyridium Cruentum*), it strengthens cellular cell-wall cohesion, sealing in moisture and reinforcing capillary resilience against daytime flushing.

4. Encapsulated Broad-Spectrum SPF 30 Matrix

Standard chemical sunscreens often migrate into eyes or trigger contact tingling. ELEMIS encapsulates its photostable UVA/UVB filters within an ultra-fine lipid-water emulsion. Upon application, the water phase evaporates into an invisible, non-greasy shield that locks UV filters onto the stratum corneum surface without penetrating into live mucosal membranes.

Frequently Asked Questions: Marine Collagen & SPF 30

Does ELEMIS Pro-Collagen Marine Cream SPF 30 leave a white cast or cause makeup pilling?

No. Unlike mineral sunscreens containing zinc oxide or titanium dioxide that can leave a chalky film, ELEMIS utilizes next-generation encapsulated chemical UV filters suspended in a weightless marine gel-cream matrix. It absorbs clear in seconds with 0% white cast across all Fitzpatrick skin types and doubles as an ultra-smooth primer beneath liquid foundations.

Can this cream replace both my daily anti-aging moisturizer and my sunscreen?

Yes. It is formulated as a complete two-in-one anti-wrinkle day cream and broad-spectrum SPF 30 sunscreen. Clinical corneometry proves it increases skin hydration by up to +253% in 1 hour while providing certified photoprotection against UVA (photoaging) and UVB (burning) rays.

How does Padina Pavonica algae firm skin and reduce wrinkles?

Padina Pavonica is a unique brown fan macroalgae harvested from the Mediterranean Sea. Rich in polysaccharides and marine minerals (calcium and magnesium), it stimulates dermal fibroblasts to synthesize glycosaminoglycans (GAGs) and collagen fibers, replenishing extracellular volume and reducing wrinkle depth within 14 days.

Does ELEMIS Marine Cream SPF 30 cause ocular burning or eye stinging?

In our laboratory ocular migration trials, the encapsulated sunscreen filters remained fixed within the lipid-cream emulsion, resulting in 0% ocular migration or lacrimation under high heat and humidity conditions. However, for direct periorbital application up to the lash line, we recommend pairing with the ophthalmologist-tested ELEMIS Pro-Collagen Eye Revive Mask.
