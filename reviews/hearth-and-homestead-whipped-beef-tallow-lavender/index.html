Dermatologist AM/PM Application Protocol

  • Evening Barrier Lock (PM): Cleanse gently, leave skin slightly damp, and press a pea-sized amount of warmed tallow balm over cheeks, neck, and dry patches.
  • Daytime UV Pairing (AM): If using in the morning on dry winter skin, allow 3 minutes to absorb before applying mineral SPF like EltaMD UV Clear SPF 46.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Hearth & Homestead 100% grass-fed and finished beef tallow different?

100% grass-fed and finished tallow offers higher levels of conjugated linoleic acid (CLA) and fat-soluble Vitamins A, D, E, and K than grain-finished tallow, supporting stronger barrier repair.

Is the Lavender version safe for eczema-prone or ultra-sensitive skin?

The organic tallow base is free of synthetic preservatives and dyes, but because it includes lavender essential oil, patients with active fissured eczema should patch-test first or opt for an unscented tallow balm.

How much Hearth & Homestead Whipped Beef Tallow should I apply to my face?

Warm a small pea-sized amount between clean fingertips and press onto slightly damp skin to lock in moisture.
