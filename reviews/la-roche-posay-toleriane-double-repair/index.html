1. Primary Barrier Restorative Actives

  • Ceramide-3 (Ceramide NP): An essential lipid identical to the skin's natural intercellular cement. Ceramide-3 locks cellular moisture into the stratum corneum, preventing microscopic fissures where allergens and environmental irritants enter.

2. Hydrating & Soothing Secondary Actives

  • Squalane: A skin-identical emollient that mimics natural sebum, providing silky suppleness without weighing down pores or generating comedones.

Frequently Asked Questions About La Roche-Posay Toleriane Double Repair

Why are Ceramides crucial for a compromised skin barrier?
Ceramides comprise approximately 50% of the stratum corneum lipid bilayer. When ceramide levels drop due to environmental aggressors or harsh cleansers, moisture escapes (TEWL) and irritants penetrate. Ceramide-3 replenishes this lipid seal, locking in water and building defensive resilience.
Will the La Roche-Posay Toleriane Double Repair cause breakouts on acne-prone skin?
No. The formula is tested non-comedogenic and is 100% oil-free. It hydrates the skin without using comedogenic waxes, petrolatum, or pore-blocking triglycerides, making it safe for acne and rosacea patients.
How do I prevent the moisturizer from pilling under makeup?
Pilling typically happens when the moisturizer is applied over thick, wet serums or rubbed excessively after it has already dried down. For best results, gently pat onto clean, slightly damp skin and allow 60 seconds for complete absorption before applying sunscreen or liquid foundation.
Can I use Toleriane Double Repair both morning and night?
Yes. Toleriane Double Repair is designed for twice-daily application. In the morning, apply it before sunscreen. In the evening, apply it as your final hydrating layer or over retinol to prevent flaking and irritation.
Is this moisturizer safe around the eyes and on eczema patches?
Yes. It is ophthalmologist-tested, allergy-tested, and free of drying alcohol and fragrance, making it gentle enough for the periorbital area and sensitive eczema-prone dermal zones.
