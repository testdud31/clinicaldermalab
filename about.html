Medical & Scientific Integrity

About ClinicalDermaLab

We bridge the critical gap between marketing claims and biological reality. Our mission is to provide patients, clinicians, and consumers with transparent, scientifically grounded skincare evaluations.

Our Independent Testing Mission

The global skincare marketplace is saturated with proprietary buzzwords, pseudo-scientific claims, and paid influencer endorsements. At ClinicalDermaLab, we operate on a fundamentally different standard: rigorous clinical evaluation, molecular ingredient analysis, and statistical meta-review of tens of thousands of verified consumer experiences.

Every formula we review undergoes independent analysis for comedogenicity, barrier repair potential (Transepidermal Water Loss reduction), cosmetic elegance under UV photography, and sensitivity tolerance across delicate conditions like acne vulgaris, rosacea, and atopic eczema.

100% Unbiased Testing

We do not accept free samples, paid placements, or corporate sponsorship from cosmetic conglomerates. Products are purchased off-the-shelf anonymously.

Medical-Grade Methodology

Our review protocols are developed by board-certified dermatologists and cosmetic formulation chemists following peer-reviewed cosmetic dermatology literature.

Big Data Patient Meta-Analysis

Beyond isolated bench testing, we aggregate and categorize 40,000+ verified customer reviews per product to discover actual real-world breakout and allergy frequencies.

Full Financial Transparency

We earn affiliate commissions when you purchase through our links at zero extra cost to you, strictly separated from our editorial ratings.

Editorial Independence & Affiliate Disclosure

ClinicalDermaLab participates in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program. When readers click through to purchase products evaluated on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission.

Crucially, our testing team operates behind an impenetrable editorial firewall. Our chemists and clinicians do not know whether a product converts commercially, nor are compensation models linked to positive ratings. If a cult-favorite sunscreen pills under makeup or triggers breakout clusters in 4% of users, we publish those findings without compromise.
