Biochemical Formulation Analysis

The therapeutic efficacy of Kiehl's Since 1851 Ultra Facial Cream stems from its deliberate biomimetic architecture. Rather than relying on heavy, suffocating petrolatum bases, Kiehl's engineers a lightweight lamellar liquid-crystal emulsion that mirrors the physiological lipid composition of human skin.

4.5% Pure Plant-Derived Squalane

Squalane is the fully saturated, chemically stable derivative of squalene—a lipid that naturally comprises roughly 13% of healthy human sebum. By replenishing depleted surface lipids with a bio-identical hydrocarbon chain, it restores flexibility to the stratum corneum and prevents moisture evaporation without clogging pores or triggering fungal acne.

Glacial Glycoprotein (Pseudoalteromonas)

Sourced sustainably from Antarctic sea glaciers, this extremophilic glycoprotein acts as a biological cryoprotectant. In clinical in vitro testing, it maintains cellular membrane hydration under extreme temperature differentials and environmental stressors, shielding skin cells from dehydration in cold climates and air-conditioned interiors.

Pro-Ceramide Botanical Matrix

Infused with cold-pressed Apricot Kernel Oil, Avocado Oil, Rice Bran Oil, and Olive Fruit Oil, this complex supplies essential linoleic and oleic fatty acids. These lipids synthesize naturally into ceramides within keratinocytes, sealing intercellular junctions and preventing micro-fissuring.

Gentle Micro-Exfoliation Buffer

Trace amounts of Salicylic Acid and Citric Acid gently optimize the formulation's physiological pH (~5.5) and encourage microscopic desquamation of dead corneocytes, allowing moisture to penetrate up to 15 epidermal tiers without chemical irritation.

According to our independent lab evaluations documented in our Clinical Testing Methodology, this biomimetic design allows Kiehl's Ultra Facial Cream to achieve a non-greasy, velvety skin-feel while outperforming heavier petrolatum balms in sustained water retention.

PM

Evening Step: Nighttime Barrier Restoration

Following your evening cleanse and active treatments (retinoids, peptides, or AHAs), apply a slightly more generous quarter-sized layer of Kiehl's Ultra Facial Cream. Alternatively, for deep anti-aging firming, consider alternating nights with Beekman 1802 Milk RX Advanced Better Aging Cream or the prebiotic ceramide formula La Roche-Posay Toleriane Double Repair.

9. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does Kiehl's Ultra Facial Cream clog pores or cause breakouts? ▼
No. In clinical laboratory patch testing and independent comedogenicity assays, Kiehl's Ultra Facial Cream scored 0 to 1 on the comedogenic scale. It utilizes pure plant-derived squalane, which closely matches the skin's endogenous lipid profile and absorbs without congesting sebaceous follicular ducts.
How does Kiehl's Ultra Facial Cream achieve 72 hours of hydration? ▼
The 72-hour moisture duration is achieved through a dual-action mechanism: Glacial Glycoprotein (Pseudoalteromonas ferment extract) binds cellular water deep within the stratum corneum (up to 15 layers deep), while 4.5% squalane and pro-ceramides seal the intercellular lipid matrix to prevent trans-epidermal water loss (TEWL).
Is Kiehl's Ultra Facial Cream safe for eczema and reactive sensitive skin? ▼
Yes. Kiehl's Ultra Facial Cream has received the National Eczema Association (NEA) Seal of Acceptance. It is formulated 100% free of synthetic fragrances, drying alcohols, parabens, and dyes, making it exceptionally well tolerated by compromised and hyper-reactive skin barriers.
How long does one 4.2 oz (125 mL) jar last with daily application? ▼
The generous 4.2 fl oz (125 mL) jar provides approximately 4 to 6 months of twice-daily facial and neck application when using the dermatologist-recommended dime-sized amount per use.
