Key Clinical Takeaways (Ingredient Science)

Clinical Bioavailability & Formulation Compatibility Rules

  • •
    Lipid Ratio Preservation: Physiological barrier creams require Ceramides, Cholesterol, and Free Fatty Acids at an equimolar ratio; isolated ceramides without co-lipids do not effectively nucleate lamellar liquid crystals.
  • •
    Photostability of Mineral Zinc Oxide: Non-nano Zinc Oxide remains completely photostable under high-intensity ultraviolet radiation, unlike organic filters which undergo photo-isomerization and radical breakdown.
  • •
    Enzymatic Specificity: Papain and Subtilisin enzymes selectively digest desmoglein-1 bonds in the outer stratum disjunctum without altering lipid bilayer organization.
Photoprotective Mineral pH: Neutral (6.5 - 7.5)

Zinc Oxide (Non-Nano & Micronized)

Inorganic broad-spectrum physical filter absorbing and scattering UVA1 (340-400nm), UVA2 (320-340nm), and UVB (290-320nm). Possesses intrinsic soothing anti-inflammatory zinc ion delivery.

Therapeutic Dosing 9.0% - 15.0%
Comedogenic Score 0 (Non-Comedogenic)
HEV Blue Light Defense High (when iron-oxide paired)
Lab-Evaluated Formulations:
• UV Clear Face Sunscreen SPF 46 (9% ZnO) • Colorescience Flex SPF 50 (12% ZnO)
Lipid Bilayer Restoration pH: 4.8 - 6.0

Ceramides (NP, AP, EOP) & Phytosphingosine

Sphingolipid molecules forming 50% of the stratum corneum intercellular lamellar matrix. Ceramide NP anchors water molecules in the extracellular space while Ceramide EOP binds the cornified envelope.

Therapeutic Dosing 0.1% - 1.5%
TEWL Reduction Up to -46.5%
Clinical Indication Atopic Xerosis & Barrier Deficits
Lab-Evaluated Formulations:
• La Roche-Posay Toleriane Double Repair • ELEMIS Superfood Midnight Facial
Enzymatic Desquamation pH: 4.0 - 5.5

Tri-Enzyme Complex (Papain, Protease, Subtilisin)

Proteolytic enzyme trio cleaving desmosomal bonds between dead stratum corneum squames without acidic burning. Accelerates cellular turnover while sparing underlying dermal cellular junctions.

Turnover Acceleration +28% in 28 Days
Barrier Safety Index 100% Non-Abrasive
Skin Sensitivity Well Tolerated (Grade 0 Stinging)
Lab-Evaluated Formulations:
• ELEMIS Dynamic Resurfacing Facial Pads
Multifunctional B-Vitamin pH: 5.0 - 6.5

Niacinamide (Nicotinamide / Vitamin B3)

Coenzyme precursor for NAD+/NADH. Stimulates endogenous ceramide and free fatty acid synthesis, inhibits melanosome transfer from melanocytes to keratinocytes, and downregulates sebaceous lipogenesis.

Therapeutic Dosing 2.0% - 5.0%
Sebum Suppression -22% to -30%
Erythema Reduction Statistically Significant
Lab-Evaluated Formulations:
• UV Clear SPF 46 (5% Pure Niacinamide) • La Roche-Posay Toleriane (4% Niacinamide)
Antioxidant & Collagen Cofactor pH: 2.8 - 3.2

L-Ascorbic Acid (Pure Vitamin C)

Potent aqueous phase antioxidant neutralizing superoxide and singlet oxygen radicals. Essential enzymatic cofactor for lysyl and prolyl hydroxylase, driving procollagen polypeptide folding.

Therapeutic Dosing 10.0% - 20.0%
Photoprotection Boost 8-Fold ROS Suppression
Stability Window Airless / Dark Amber Amber Vial
Lab-Evaluated Formulations:
• Obagi Medical Professional-C 20% Serum
Glycosaminoglycan Stimulator pH: 5.0 - 6.5

Padina Pavonica (Brown Mediterranean Algae)

Calcified brown marine algae rich in polysaccharides and calcium ions. Stimulates synthesis of dermal glycosaminoglycans (GAGs) like hyaluronic acid in dermal fibroblasts, restoring turgor.

Target Mechanism Fibroblast GAG Induction
Hydration Duration 24-Hour Sustained Matrix
Clinical Indication Solar Elastosis & Skin Laxity
Lab-Evaluated Formulations:
• ELEMIS Pro-Collagen Marine Cream SPF 30 • ELEMIS Pro-Collagen Cleansing Balm
