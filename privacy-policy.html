ClinicalDermaLab Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 14, 2026

1. Commitment to Reader Privacy

At ClinicalDermaLab ("we", "our", or "the Platform"), we respect your personal privacy. We maintain a strict policy of data minimization: we only collect the minimum information required to deliver high-performance dermatological reviews, process reader inquiries, and monitor site reliability. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party data brokers or marketing firms.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information in the following ways:

  • Direct Submissions: When you contact our editorial desk via our contact form (Contact Us) or email, we collect your name, email address, and the content of your message to respond to your inquiry.
  • Log and Device Data: Like most web servers, our infrastructure automatically records non-identifiable technical data including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, and page request timestamps to secure our platform against abuse.
  • Cookies & Local Storage: We use essential cookies to maintain page preferences, filter settings in our review directory, and monitor site uptime.

3. Affiliate Disclosure & Commercial Links

ClinicalDermaLab is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for websites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com.

When you click on outbound product links or "Check Price & Verified Ratings on Amazon" buttons, Amazon may place an affiliate cookie in your browser to attribute qualifying purchases. These cookies do not give us access to your personal payment information, full billing details, or physical address.

For more information on how Amazon handles your purchasing data, please consult the Amazon Privacy Notice.

4. Use of Information

The information we collect is utilized strictly to:

  • Provide, maintain, and optimize the ClinicalDermaLab platform.
  • Respond to editorial feedback, reader questions, or clinical corrections.
  • Analyze aggregated traffic patterns and review engagement without identifying individual users.
  • Detect, prevent, and mitigate security threats, spam, or malicious bot activities.

5. GDPR and CCPA/CPRA Compliance Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or California), you are entitled to specific legal rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: You may request confirmation of whether we process personal data relating to you and receive a copy.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request the deletion of your personal communications from our records.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, please email our Data Privacy Officer directly at privacy@clinicaldermalab.com.

6. Third-Party Web Links

Our clinical reviews and comparison tables include hyperlinks to external scientific databases (e.g., PubMed, Journal of the American Academy of Dermatology) and retail websites. We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party domains.

7. Data Security

We employ industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / SSL) encryption across all endpoints. While no electronic transmission over the internet is completely infallible, we maintain strict administrative and technological controls to safeguard communications.

8. Contact the Privacy Team

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or our data management procedures, please contact us:
