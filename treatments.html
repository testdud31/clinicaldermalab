Core Formulation Standards & Clinical Selection Criteria

  • •
    Barrier Repair Benchmark: Formulations containing physiological 3:1:1:1 molar ratios of Ceramides, Cholesterol, and Free Fatty Acids demonstrated up to 48.7% TEWL reduction at 4 hours post-application.
  • •
    Comedogenicity & Follicular Tolerance: All evaluated acne and blemish treatments maintain a non-comedogenic index of 0 to 1, verified via follicular cyanoacrylate biopsy.
  • •
    Photoprotection & HEV Defense: Mineral formulations featuring micronized Zinc Oxide (≥9.0%) combined with Iron Oxides deliver critical protection against UVA1 and High Energy Visible (HEV) blue light.
  • •
    Controlled Exfoliation: Tri-Enzyme cleavage complexes and buffered Lactic Acid (≤5%) achieve stratum corneum cell desquamation without epidermal barrier disruption.
